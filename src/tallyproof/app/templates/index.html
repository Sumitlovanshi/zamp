Every number on this receipt is proven, repaired, or honestly flagged.

OCR tools hand you a table that looks right and a confidence score that means nothing. Tallyproof reads the receipt's own arithmetic — the line chain, the totals block, the cash anchor, the item count — and enumerates every reading the glyphs admit. One explanation: certified. Several: it shows you all of them. None: the paper itself doesn't add up, and that's proven, not guessed. There is no confidence threshold anywhere in the system.

What it refuses to do

  • No confidence scores. A verdict is a cardinality — how many readings survive — never a 0.94 that means nothing.
  • No repair is ever silent. The original stays on screen next to what arithmetic says it must have been.
  • What arithmetic can't reach, it won't decorate. On the evaluation corpus ~40% of cells end up outside PROVEN — merchant names, dates, unanchored numbers — each labelled as exactly what it is.
  • Bank statements, invoices, handwriting: out of scope. Deliberately — the decisions log says why.
